Four satellites, two pixel sizes

MODIS has watched Brazil in 1 km pixels from November 2000. VIIRS joined in 2012 with 375 m pixels, so it sees smaller fires and records more hotspots for the same burning.

VIIRS S-NPP stopped on 2 November 2026.
VIIRS NOAA-20 is the scale every count is converted to.

Questions

About the data, the method and the downloads.

Where does the data come from?

Only NASA FIRMS: MODIS 6.1 and VIIRS 375 m hotspots. The map imagery is not used in any number.

What is a fire-day?

A grid cell of about 1 km with at least one valid hotspot on one UTC day.

Why are recent calendar months hatched?

They use near-real-time data, which NASA replaces with final data about five months later.

Which areas does the calendar cover?

Four presets, a rectangle or polygon you draw, or a point with a radius.

Does the calendar forecast fires?

No. The coming-months card shows past years and says it is not a forecast.

The method in 3 steps

1

Counts fire-days on a 1 km grid

Many hotspots in one cell on one day count once, so a sharper sensor cannot inflate the total.

7 fire-days from 23 hotspots
2

Converts each satellite to NOAA-20

Months seen by two satellites at once set the conversion, and held-out years test it.
